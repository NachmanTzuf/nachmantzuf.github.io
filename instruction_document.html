CRITICAL ENGINE BEHAVIOR: TONIC RESOLUTION MECHANISM
In functional ear training, after the user submits their answer for a target note, the app does not just mark correct/wrong: it plays the melodic resolution back to the nearest tonic degree (e.g. Re (2) resolves down to Do (1); Ti (7) resolves upward to High Do (8); Fa (4) resolves down to Mi (3) then Do (1)). The implementing agent must preserve this acoustic resolution pipeline.

The implementing agent must systematically process all files across the three Desktop subdirectories:

  • 1.1 The Major Scale (Diatonic): Starts with partial subsets (e.g. notes 1-4: C, D, E, F), transitions to upper tetrachord (notes 5-8: G, A, B, C), full 1-octave, multi-octave, and transposed (Not-C major).

3. Architecture Extension 1: Gentle Two-Note Entry Foundation

In the original application, the very first level begins with 4 notes (Do, Re, Mi, Fa). User feedback confirms this is an intimidating leap for absolute beginners. The new application must prepend a dedicated Stage 0: Gentle Two-Note Foundation before the legacy curriculum begins.

Stage Level Level Name Active Degrees Educational & Acoustic Rationale
Level 0.1 Bilateral Poles (Do & High Do) Degree 1 (Do) & Degree 8 (High Do) Extreme register difference. Easiest acoustic anchor to distinguish; establishes the octave frame.
Level 0.2 The Gravitational Fifth Degree 1 (Do) & Degree 5 (Sol) The most acoustically stable consonant interval in the harmonic series. Grounding the dominant.
Level 0.3 Stepwise Gravity (Do & Re) Degree 1 (Do) & Degree 2 (Re) Introduces adjacent whole-step tension. Re clearly resolves down to Do.
Level 0.4 Leading Tone Magnet (Do & Ti) Degree 1 (Do) & Degree 7 (Ti) Introduces half-step upward tension. Ti clearly resolves upward into High Do.
Level 0.5 The Major Triad Core Degree 1 (Do), 3 (Mi), 5 (Sol) Expands to 3 consonant triad tones, bridging seamlessly into the legacy 4-note starting point.

4. Architecture Extension 2: Advanced Harmonic Dyads (30-Level Roadmap)

In the original app, "Advanced" was merely an unstructured custom sandbox. In our production application, Advanced is elevated to a premier Harmonic Dyad Training Track where the engine plays two notes simultaneously (harmonic intervals / chords) for the user to decipher.

Phase Level Range Intervals Tested Pedagogical Strategy & Octaves
Phase 1: Open Consonances Levels Adv-01 to Adv-06 Unisons (1-1), Octaves (1-8), Perfect Fifths (1-5), Perfect Fourths (1-4). 1 octave. Clean hollow resonances with zero beating. The user identifies open hollow acoustic intervals.
Phase 2: Sweet Consonances Levels Adv-07 to Adv-12 Major Thirds (1-3), Minor Thirds (1-b3), Major Sixths (1-6), Minor Sixths (1-b6). 1 octave. Warm triad colorations. Introduces major vs minor harmonic dualities over tonic pedals.
Phase 3: Mild Dissonances Levels Adv-13 to Adv-18 Major Seconds (1-2), Minor Sevenths (1-b7), Suspended Dyads (2-5, 4-5). 1 octave. Acoustic friction and gentle beating frequencies. Focuses on resolving energy.
Phase 4: Sharp Dissonances Levels Adv-19 to Adv-24 Tritones (1-#4/b5), Minor Seconds (1-b2), Major Sevenths (1-7). 1 octave. Extreme acoustic tension. Distinguishing half-step harshness from tritone instability.
Phase 5: Multi-Octave Open Dyads Levels Adv-25 to Adv-30 Compound Major/Minor 10ths, Compound 12ths (Octave + 5th), Inverted Dyads. Many octaves. Wide pitch spreads across harp registers, demanding spatial acoustic recognition.
