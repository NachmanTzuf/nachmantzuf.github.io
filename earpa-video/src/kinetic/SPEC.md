# Earpa kinetic rebuild — four focused videos
Autonomous creation, following the user's explicit motion direction. Existing Remotion project retained because V5/V6 assets and source are Remotion. Local video-shotcraft recipes and HyperFrames motion craft consulted.

## Scope
One 45s horizontal ad, one 45s vertical ad, one 40s single-note tutorial, one 90s lever masterclass. The latest four-video scope supersedes the earlier request for both 30s/45s variants. No bulk variant render.

## Reference breakdown
V5: large independently moving harp, rotational reveals, clean dark stage, app proof and 3D-path finish. V6: preserve the actual existing voice recording and complete wording. Voice generator: en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural, clause-dependent speed/pitch, 90ms breaths, 75Hz highpass, 170Hz warmth, 6200Hz notch, gentle saturation, falling final Earpa. Original voice is reused, not regenerated for ads.

## Motion and design
Emerald/ink background, antique gold, cream type. Ads change visual emphasis every 0.8–2.3s; 6-frame crash push, 12-frame perspective handoffs, 3-frame word staggering; no sine-floating stand-in for choreography. Hold the final brand 2s. Tutorial listening passages retain real cadence timing; teaching motion must not obscure listening or leak the mystery note.
Recipe adaptation: crash-zoom-punch / CrashZoomReal.tsx (6f attack, restrained settle); word-relay-geometry for three-step loop; perspective product orbit follows V5. User's explicit fast pace overrides skill R3 default slow pacing. No added music bed: original V6 mix retained; tutorial contains instructional sound and tactile SFX only.

## Full-bleed phones
Actual clean captures are used for gameplay/home. Legacy screen assets include baked bezels; clip those away before fitting to the single outer phone. Never objectFit contain. Screen rectangle and image have identical aspect framing; only a slim device rim remains. UI never appears as a floating unframed screenshot.

## Tutorial truth
Cadence and resolution notes come from CadenceEngine and ResolutionWalker; pluck samples come from HarpSoundSynthesizer.generatePluckedStringSamples with seeded randomness. Demonstration is a timed reconstruction with genuine captured app imagery, not a live recording. Two cases: D/degree 2 guessed correctly, F/degree 4 guessed as E/degree 3 incorrectly. Real I–IV–V–I, auditory-only mystery note, selection, correct-answer feedback, then 2→1 or 4→3→2→1 at the app's cadence beat.
