## 1. CORE BRAND VALUES & TRUTH-IN-ADVERTISING COMMITMENTS

1. **Zero Fabricated Claims:**
   - No fake user metrics or claims of tens of thousands of active users.
   - Honest positioning: "Built specifically for Celtic lever harpers by dedicated musicians and lutherie engineers."
2. **Honest Pedagogical Claims:**
   - Never claim "effortless perfect pitch in 3 days."
   - Frame the method accurately: "Functional ear training teaches you to perceive scale degrees by their gravitational pull toward the tonic home, rather than calculating intervals."
3. **Real In-App Behavior:**
   - All video demonstrations and screen recordings must show the real software interface:
     - I - IV - V - I voice-leading cadence.
     - Pure auditory stimulus playback (zero visual light leakage).
     - Stepwise closest-C resolution walks (down to 1 or up to 8 at unhurried singing pace).
     - Accurate sharping lever flips with authentic pitch shifts.

## 2. KEY VALUE PROPOSITIONS

| Feature | The Problem Harpers Face | How Earpa Solves It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Functional Tonal Gravity** | Most ear trainers play random notes in a vacuum, teaching isolated interval math. | Earpa anchors every trial in a rich I - IV - V - I cadence, training your ear to feel each note's desire to resolve home. |
| **Celtic Lever Lutherie** | Piano-centric apps ignore the reality of sharping levers, open tunings, and accidentals. | Native support for E♭, C, and B♭ tunings with physical 3D sharping levers, circle of fifths math, and octave rules. |
| **Physical String Plucking** | Tapping abstract numbers on a screen disconnects ear training from instrument muscle memory. | Direct finger plucking on the 3D Celtic harp strings, producing instant acoustic plucks and synchronized resolution walks. |
